Degraded synchronization throughput is frequently attributable to one of several underlying conditions. The most prevalent cause is insufficient batch sizing, whereby the configured batch parameter results in an excessive number of round trips to the database. Alternatively, network latency between your compute environment and the S3 endpoint may be contributing substantially to overall duration, particularly where the bucket has been provisioned in a geographically distant region. It is also advisable to examine whether the source tables have acquired additional indexes, since index maintenance can materially impact read performance. Finally, verify that concurrent synchronization operations are not competing for the same database connections, as connection pool exhaustion manifests as generalized slowness.
