In the event that synchronization has ceased and the pending queue is exhibiting sustained growth, the initial diagnostic step involves interrogating the service status endpoint to determine whether the worker process remains operational. Should the worker be non-responsive, restarting the service is generally sufficient to restore normal operation. If the worker is operational but not progressing, examine the application logs for authentication or permission failures, which frequently occur subsequent to credential rotation. Where the queue depth exceeds ten thousand items, it is advisable to temporarily increase worker concurrency in order to facilitate recovery, and to notify the data platform team that downstream freshness guarantees may be compromised.
