Between 02:10 and 06:20 UTC, tablesync persisted incomplete object payloads to S3 for a subset of configured tables. The precipitating factor was a network configuration change which reduced the maximum transmission unit on the egress path, resulting in the truncation of larger multipart uploads without a corresponding error being surfaced to the application layer. Because the upload completion handler interpreted the truncated response as successful, no retry was initiated and downstream consumers subsequently ingested partial datasets. Remediation involved reverting the network change and reprocessing the affected window. We are implementing checksum validation on upload completion in order to ensure that comparable failures are detected proactively.
