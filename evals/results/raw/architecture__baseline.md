tablesync operates according to a three-stage pipeline architecture. The reader stage establishes a connection to PostgreSQL and executes a cursor-based query, which permits traversal of arbitrarily large tables without materializing the complete result set in memory. Retrieved rows are subsequently forwarded to the batching stage, which accumulates records until either a configurable size threshold is attained or a timeout elapses, whereupon the accumulated batch is serialized into the designated output format. The writer stage thereafter performs a multipart upload to S3, employing exponential backoff in the event of transient failures. Stage isolation is maintained through bounded channels, which provides natural backpressure when the writer is unable to keep pace with the reader.
